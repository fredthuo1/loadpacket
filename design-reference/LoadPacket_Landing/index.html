A few things to know.

Do I need a rate confirmation to start?

No. You can upload a rate confirmation or enter the load manually. Both lead into the same load workflow.

Will I review the extracted details?

Yes. Check and correct the extracted information before creating the load. You stay in control of the final details.

Is the preview showing live company data?

No. The interactive preview uses fictional sample data to show how a load, its documents and billing can stay connected.
